4. Payment and delivery

  • Prices are in Turkish lira and include VAT.
  • Payment is taken by credit or debit card in a single payment through İyzi Ödeme ve Elektronik Para Hizmetleri A.Ş. (iyzico). Card details are not passed to or stored by the Seller.
  • Credits are added electronically to the Buyer’s Platform account as soon as the payment is approved. There is no physical delivery.
  • The invoice is issued as an e-Fatura for Buyers registered for e-Fatura, otherwise as an e-Arşiv invoice, and is sent to the Buyer’s email address.

5. Right of withdrawal

  • Because credits are added to the account as soon as the payment is approved, and performance of the service begins immediately with the Buyer’s approval, there is no right of withdrawal under Article 15(1)(g) and (ğ) of the Regulation on Distance Contracts.
  • Nevertheless, the Seller grants a Buyer who is a consumer the following option: if no credit has been used on the account since the purchase (no photo uploaded), the Buyer may withdraw from the agreement within 14 (fourteen) days of its conclusion, without giving a reason and without penalty.
  • Notice of withdrawal is given within that period by email to pinar@slothcoffeeshop.com, stating the order number.
  • Within 14 (fourteen) days of receiving the notice, the Seller refunds the full amount to the card used for payment, at no cost, and removes the related credits from the account.
  • Once credits start being used on the account after the purchase, this withdrawal option ends. No partial refunds are made.

6. Functionality and compatibility of the digital service

The Platform is used with a current web browser on any device with an internet connection; no software needs to be installed. Access is by signing in with a one-time code sent by SMS to the user’s mobile phone number.

7. Placing the order and keeping the agreement

  • The order is placed by choosing a credit package on the Platform, entering billing details, approving this form and the Distance Sales Agreement, and completing the payment on the iyzico payment page.
  • Before going to the payment page, the Buyer can see and correct the chosen package and the billing details.
  • This form and the Distance Sales Agreement are sent to the Buyer’s email address and kept available in the Buyer’s Platform account. The Seller keeps order records for the period required by law.
  • The language of the agreement is Turkish. The English translation is for information only.
  • Personal data is processed in accordance with the Privacy Notice published on the Platform.

8. Complaints and disputes

The Buyer can send requests and complaints to the Seller using the contact details in Section 1. A Buyer who is a consumer may apply, within the monetary limits announced each year by the Ministry of Trade, to the Consumer Arbitration Committee where they live or where the consumer transaction took place, and to the Consumer Court for disputes above those limits. Applications to the Consumer Arbitration Committee can also be made on e-Devlet through the Consumer Information System (TÜBİS).
