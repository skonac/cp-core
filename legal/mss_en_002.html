Article 2 — Definitions

  • Platform: the Clever Photos service provided through the cleverphotos.org website and its related applications.
  • Credit: a digital usage unit used to upload event photos to the Platform. 1 credit entitles the holder to upload 1 photo.
  • Credit package: a package containing a set number of credits, offered for sale on the Platform.
  • Consumer: a natural or legal person acting for non-commercial and non-professional purposes, as defined in Consumer Protection Law No. 6502.
  • Regulation: the Turkish Regulation on Distance Contracts (Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği).

This agreement sets out the rights and obligations of the parties, under Law No. 6502 and the Regulation, for the sale of the credit package that the Buyer ordered electronically on the Platform, as described below with its price, and for the crediting of that package to the Buyer’s account.

Article 5 — Delivery and use of credits

  1. Credits are added to the Buyer’s Platform account as soon as the payment institution approves the payment. There is no physical delivery.
  2. 1 credit is deducted from the Buyer’s account for each photo uploaded to and accepted by the Platform. Credits are not returned for photos that are later deleted or that contain no faces.
  3. Credits cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to another account. Except as set out in Articles 7 and 8, the price of credits is not refunded.
  4. If no credit is used and no new credit package is bought on the account for 3 (three) years, the remaining credits on the account expire at the end of that period.
  5. A current web browser and an internet connection are sufficient to use the Platform. The rules for using the Platform are set out in the Terms of Use published on the Platform.

Article 6 — Price, payment and invoice

  1. Prices are in Turkish lira and include VAT. The price at the time of the order applies.
  2. Payment is taken by card in a single payment through İyzi Ödeme ve Elektronik Para Hizmetleri A.Ş. (iyzico). Card details are not passed to or stored by the Seller.
  3. The invoice is issued with the billing details the Buyer provides: as an e-Fatura for Buyers registered for e-Fatura, otherwise as an e-Arşiv invoice. It is sent to the Buyer’s email address. The Buyer is responsible for the accuracy of the billing details.

Article 7 — Right of withdrawal

  1. With the approval the Buyer gives during payment, credits are added to the Buyer’s account and performance of the service begins as soon as the payment is approved. Under Article 15(1)(g) and (ğ) of the Regulation, there is no right of withdrawal for services performed instantly in electronic form or for services whose performance has begun with the consumer’s approval.
  2. Nevertheless, the Seller grants a Buyer who is a consumer the following option: if no credit has been used on the Buyer’s account since the purchase (no photo uploaded), the Buyer may withdraw from this agreement within 14 (fourteen) days of its conclusion, without giving a reason and without penalty.
  3. Notice of withdrawal is given within that period by email to pinar@slothcoffeeshop.com, stating the order number.
  4. Within 14 (fourteen) days of receiving the notice, the Seller refunds the full amount charged to the card used for payment, at no cost to the Buyer, and removes the package’s credits from the account. The time it takes for the refund to appear on the card depends on the Buyer’s bank.
  5. Once credits start being used on the account after the purchase, this withdrawal option ends regardless of how many credits were used. No partial refunds are made.

Article 8 — End of service

  1. If the Seller decides to close the Platform permanently, it will announce this at least 30 (thirty) days in advance on the Platform and by email to the Buyer. In that case, the price of the unused and unexpired credits remaining on the Buyer’s account is refunded at the unit price at which they were bought.
  2. If the Buyer closes their account at their own request, no refund is made for credits remaining on the account.

Article 9 — Obligations of the parties

  1. The Seller provides the Platform with reasonable care and in accordance with this agreement. Temporary interruptions caused by planned maintenance, failures of infrastructure providers or force majeure are not a breach of this agreement.
  2. The Buyer confirms that the information given in the order is correct, that they hold the rights and permissions needed for the photos they upload to the Platform, and that they are responsible for informing the people in those photos that the photos will be shared on the Platform.

Article 10 — Personal data

The Buyer’s personal data is processed in accordance with the Privacy Notice published on the Platform.

Article 11 — Complaints and disputes

  1. The Buyer can send requests and complaints to the Seller using the contact details in Article 1.1.
  2. A Buyer who is a consumer may apply, within the monetary limits announced each year by the Ministry of Trade, to the Consumer Arbitration Committee where they live or where the consumer transaction took place, and to the Consumer Court for disputes above those limits. Applications to the Consumer Arbitration Committee can also be made on e-Devlet through the Consumer Information System (TÜBİS).
  3. For disputes with Buyers who are not consumers, the courts and enforcement offices of Ankara have jurisdiction.

Article 12 — Entry into force

  1. This agreement is concluded when the Buyer approves the Preliminary Information Form and this agreement electronically on the Platform and the payment is completed.
  2. The agreement and the Preliminary Information Form are sent to the Buyer’s email address and kept available in the Buyer’s Platform account. The Seller keeps order records for the period required by law.
  3. The Turkish text of this agreement prevails. This English translation is for information only.
