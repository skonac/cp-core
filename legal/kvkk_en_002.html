Part A — Privacy Notice

Clever Photos is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: pinar@slothcoffeeshop.com, +90 538 417 56 84.

2. What we collect

  • Account: mobile phone number, name (if you give one), your roles (guest / organizer).
  • Face data: the selfies you upload and the face templates (numeric vectors) we compute from them. This is biometric data, a special category under KVKK, and is only processed with your explicit consent (Part B).
  • Event data: events you join, photos matched to you, downloads and purchases.
  • Organizer data: billing details, credit purchases, invoices, and the event photos you upload.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser details, access and security logs (e.g. SMS code attempts).
  • Payments: card payments are taken by İyzi Ödeme ve Elektronik Para Hizmetleri A.Ş. (iyzico). We never see or store card numbers.

4. Who we share it with

  • Our hosting provider, Hetzner Online GmbH (servers in the European Union).
  • An SMS service provider (phone number only, to send codes).
  • The payment institution (iyzico), e-invoice and accounting service providers.
  • The event organizer, who sees that you joined their event. Organizers do not see your selfies or face templates.
  • Courts and public authorities, when the law requires it.

5. Transfers abroad

Our servers are outside Türkiye, in the European Union. Transfers are made under Art. 9 KVKK (as amended by Law No. 7499), using the standard contracts announced by the Personal Data Protection Board, notified to the Authority within 5 business days of signing.

6. How long we keep it

  • Selfies and face templates: until you delete them or your account. When you delete a selfie, the file and the face templates computed from it are erased at once.
  • Event photos and matches: until the event is ended, at most 12 months after the event date.
  • Account data: while your account is open; afterwards only what the law requires (e.g. invoice and sales records, up to 10 years).
  • Access logs: 2 years, as required by Law No. 5651 and related regulations.

7. People who appear in event photos

Organizers upload their event photos. To make matching work, the system detects every face in those photos, including people who never sign up, and computes face templates. These are compared only within that one event, only against guests who gave consent, and are never used to identify anyone by name. The organizer is responsible for telling attendees that photos will be shared on Clever Photos. Anyone may ask us to remove photos or face data about them.

8. Your rights

Under Art. 11 KVKK you may ask whether we process your data and get information about it; learn the purpose and whether it is used for that purpose; know who it is shared with in Türkiye or abroad; have it corrected, deleted or destroyed, and have those changes passed on to recipients; object to a result against you that comes solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing.

Apply in writing to Güvenlik Caddesi No: 36A, Ayrancı, Çankaya / Ankara, or from your registered phone number or email to pinar@slothcoffeeshop.com. We reply within 30 days, free of charge in principle. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Board.

Part B — Explicit Consent for Face Data

Clever Photos can find the photos you appear in. To do this we process your selfies and the face templates computed from them (biometric data) as described in Part A.

  • Purpose: only to match your face against photos of events you join. Never for advertising, profiling or identifying you elsewhere.
  • Optional: you can use Clever Photos without it. You won’t get face matching, but, if the organizer allows it, you can still browse all photos of the event.
  • Withdraw any time: delete your face photos in My face, or delete your account; your face templates are erased at once. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it.

I have read the Privacy Notice and give my explicit consent to Clever Photos processing my face photos and the face templates computed from them, to find photos of me in the events I join.
